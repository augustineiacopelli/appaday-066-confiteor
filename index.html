Telling venial from mortal
The Church names three conditions for a mortal sin: it must be grave matter, done with full knowledge that it is seriously wrong, and with the deliberate consent of your will. If even one condition is missing, the sin is venial - real and worth confessing, but it wounds your friendship with God rather than severing it. Grave matter alone does not make a sin mortal; a person can lack full knowledge or full freedom. Confess mortal sins in kind and, as best you can recall, in number, and before receiving Communion again. When you are honestly unsure, name it and let the priest help you weigh it. Scruples and despair are not from God - his mercy is greater than any sin.

The Rite of Penance

Going to Confession

If it has been a long time, that is exactly what this is for. Just tell the priest how long, and that you may need help with the words. He has heard everything, and he is glad you came.

Examine your conscience

Before you enter, spend a few quiet minutes with the examination. Ask the Holy Spirit to show you honestly.

Greet the priest

Make the Sign of the Cross with him, then open with the traditional greeting.

Bless me, Father, for I have sinned. It has been ___ since my last confession.

Confess your sins

Tell him your sins simply and honestly. Name mortal sins in kind and number as best you recall. Start with what weighs most.

Listen and receive your penance

The priest may offer a word of counsel, then give you a penance - usually a prayer or an act to complete afterward.

Pray the Act of Contrition

When he asks, express your sorrow. You may use your own words or a traditional prayer.

O my God, I am heartily sorry for having offended Thee...

Receive absolution

The priest extends his hand and absolves you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Answer "Amen" and make the Sign of the Cross.

Give thanks, then do your penance

He will dismiss you: "Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good." Answer: "His mercy endures forever." Leave in peace and complete your penance soon.
